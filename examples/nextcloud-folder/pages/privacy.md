## Comments

Comments are written with Remark42, which runs on [YOUR COMMENTS ADDRESS] next
to this website. To comment you log in with your email address: you get a link
by mail. Reading comments needs no login.

When you log in and comment, the following is stored: your name, your comment
with the time, the page, and what Remark42 needs to recognise you. After the
login your browser keeps two cookies (`JWT` and `XSRF-TOKEN`) so that you stay
logged in. Mails are sent through [YOUR MAIL PROVIDER].

## Embedded videos

Videos from YouTube are not loaded when you open a page. They load only after
you click "Load content", from the privacy-enhanced domain
`youtube-nocookie.com`. From that moment YouTube receives your IP address.
